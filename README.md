# Bee2Boss

**Plan your move from W-2 to Self-Employed.**

A free, guided planner for anyone thinking about leaving a W-2 job to work for themselves as a consultant or freelancer.

It takes you through five short steps:

1. **Basics:** who you are, what you'd sell, and when you'd leave, plus your paycheck today.
2. **Your rate:** a slider game. Fill the bar past your take-home today and unlock milestones at +15%, +30% and +50%.
3. **What's next:** your runway, meaning the savings you need to get through the wait for your first client payment.
4. **Plan:** a launch checklist dated back from your last day.
5. **Q&A:** questions to take to your CPA, attorney, first client and insurance agent. It also has a "Copy my plan for AI" button that builds a summary to paste into Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser. There's nothing to install and no account, and nothing you type leaves your device. Your plan is saved in your browser, and you can **Save a copy** as a file.

Want to look around first? Pick **See it with Sam first** to explore with Sam, a made-up operations manager.

## What the math covers

- 2026 federal tax brackets for a **single filer**, with the $16,100 standard deduction
- Self-employment tax, the half-SE deduction and the self-employed health insurance deduction
- Solo 401(k) limits, including the catch-up for ages 50+ and 60-63
- The 20% QBI deduction, which phases out above about $200k of taxable income
- State tax as one flat rate that you set

**Not modeled:** married filing jointly, S-corps, state credits, and the extra deductions at age 65+.

## Disclaimer

This is a planning tool. It isn't tax, legal or financial advice. Have a CPA check your numbers before you make a decision.
